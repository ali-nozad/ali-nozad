## How I work

1. **Start with the operation, not the model.** I map the real workflow, constraints, users, and success criteria first.
2. **Design for production.** Reliability, security, error recovery, monitoring, and cost are part of the architecture.
3. **Ship, measure, and improve.** I turn prototypes into usable products and iterate from real behavior and feedback.
